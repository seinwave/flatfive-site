FlatFive does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.
{: .lede }

## What is collected

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash
reporting, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

## Network use

FlatFive makes no network requests. It works identically with the device in airplane mode,
because it never needs to reach anything.

## What is stored, and where

Your settings and saved presets are written to your device's own storage, in the app's
private container. They never leave the device except as part of your own device backup, if
you have backups switched on. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Children

Because the app collects no data at all, it collects none from children either.

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date
