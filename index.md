## What it does

- **Every chord type you actually read** — triads through sevenths, extensions and altered
  dominants, in every common spelling: `m7` and `-7`, `maj7` and `Δ7`, `ø7` and `m7♭5`.
- **Two ways to move** — on the metronome's downbeat, or by tapping Next when you are ready.
- **Weighted keys** — spend more time in the keys you are shaky in, and none in the ones you
  are not.
- **Voicings and hands** — ask for a specific inversion, or which hand to play with.
- **Presets** — save a way of working and switch between them.
- **Timed sessions** — the drill stops itself when the time is up.

Nothing is collected, nothing is sent anywhere, and it works in airplane mode. See the
[privacy policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).
